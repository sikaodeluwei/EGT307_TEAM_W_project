# EGT307_TEAM_W_project
Week 14 Project Planning
1. Project Title
AutoCare AI: Smart Vehicle Maintenance Prediction System

2. Project Domain
Machine Learning and Automation

3. Problem Statement
Vehicle owners may not always recognise early warning signs of vehicle maintenance problems. Issues such as low battery voltage, abnormal engine temperature, poor tyre pressure, worn brake components, or overdue engine oil may be ignored until a serious breakdown occurs.
Unexpected vehicle breakdowns may result in repair costs, safety risks, and inconvenience to vehicle owners. Therefore, the group proposes an AI-based vehicle maintenance prediction system that analyses vehicle condition data and predicts whether maintenance is required.

4. Industry Relevance
The project is relevant to the automotive and transport engineering industries because regular vehicle maintenance is important for vehicle reliability, safety, and operating efficiency.
The proposed application can help users detect possible maintenance needs earlier. This allows vehicle owners, technicians, and fleet operators to carry out preventive maintenance before a serious failure occurs.
The project also supports better maintenance planning by converting vehicle data into a simple risk level and recommendation.

5. Project Objectives
To develop an AI model that predicts the maintenance condition of a vehicle.
To classify the vehicle condition as Healthy, Maintenance Soon, or Immediate Service Required.
To identify vehicle factors that may contribute to maintenance problems.
To provide simple maintenance recommendations based on the predicted condition.
To develop the application using a modular microservices architecture.

6. Target Users
The target users are:
Private vehicle owners
Vehicle maintenance technicians
Workshop staff
Delivery and transport companies
Fleet maintenance managers

7. Expected Outcomes
The expected outcome is a functional AI application that accepts vehicle condition data and predicts the required maintenance level.
The system is expected to provide:
Vehicle maintenance prediction
Maintenance risk level
Prediction confidence score
Possible maintenance issue
Suggested maintenance action
Storage of past prediction records

8. Intended Benefits
The proposed system may provide the following benefits:
Early identification of maintenance problems
Reduced risk of unexpected vehicle breakdown
Improved vehicle safety
Better preventive maintenance planning
Reduced long-term repair costs
Easier interpretation of vehicle condition data
